Physical Magnetism Consulting

A mirror, not a verdict. Turn your felt, sensory data into conscious trajectories you can act on.

The Main Workflow: Your Data-Somatic Journey

Every decision is an act of sorting. Traditional frameworks leave out most of what you actually feel, dismissing gut and body signals as noise. The Physical Magnetism workflow puts your felt data back into the calculation through a private, browser-based, interactive mirror.

Step 1: The Pluriverse Journal

Hold it & Name it. We begin with localized, reflective text prompts designed for slow, deliberate dialogue. You enter your current state—your challenges, stuck points, and nuances—in plain text. The tool plots your feelings into a visible, color-based trajectory indicating mass (saturation), voltage (energy transitions), and essential rest ratios. Export your raw JSON data.

Step 2: The Color Plotter

Calibrate & Act. From your own words, the engine maps sentiment scores without ever storing your data. Your narrative maps directly onto La Matrice—a 15-color semantic-somatic framework. You read the sequences, choose what coheres, and author your own next steps across mind, body, and speech.
